Workshop lead, with Raymond Duch, for the afternoon session (13:30–15:30, 13 October 2026) of the LLMSS 2026 pre-conference workshop programme at City University of Hong Kong. The conference is organised by the Peking University Analytics Lab for Global Risk Politics, the City University of Hong Kong Computational Social Sciences Lab, and the *Talking to Machines* initiative (Nuffield College, University of Oxford).

The session extends the AI-polling method beyond elections: harvesting someone's public posts, repeatedly interviewing an LLM "digital twin" built from them, and applying panel econometrics to the result turns scattered, self-selected commentary into a balanced, forward-looking panel of on-demand forecasters. It covers three applications — financial markets (finfluencer twins and S&P 500 returns), forecasting and prediction markets, and the measurement of sensitive topics — with a hands-on segment on building a digital twin.
