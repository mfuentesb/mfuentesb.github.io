Workshop lead, with Raymond Duch and Laurenz Günther, for the morning session (09:00–12:30, 13 October 2026) of the LLMSS 2026 pre-conference workshop programme at City University of Hong Kong. The conference is organised by the Peking University Analytics Lab for Global Risk Politics, the City University of Hong Kong Computational Social Sciences Lab, and the *Talking to Machines* initiative (Nuffield College, University of Oxford).

A hands-on session on inferring public opinion from the digital traces people already leave online. Participants work through **PoSSUM** — the Protocol for Surveying Social Media Users with Multimodal LLMs — whose state-by-state forecasts of the 2024 US presidential election tracked, and at times outperformed, the leading poll aggregators. The session covers digital interview design, building a "silicon" sample, and producing bias-corrected estimates with multilevel regression and post-stratification (MrP) in R, with a case study on Swiss "Silicon Politicians."
