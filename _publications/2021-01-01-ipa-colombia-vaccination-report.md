abstract: >-
  This report examines Colombian public opinion on COVID-19 vaccine prioritization and distribution,
  drawing on RECOVR survey data. As of late February 2021, Colombia had recorded 2.25 million COVID-19
  cases and nearly 60,000 deaths, second only to Brazil in Latin America, and had enforced a mandatory
  national quarantine from March 16 to August 31, 2020. The report finds that one in three respondents
  had experienced reduced income by the end of the quarantine, primarily due to temporary or permanent
  workplace closures, and argues that vaccine distribution was critical both for disease prevention and
  for enabling the relaxation of containment measures. Released March 30, 2021.
